# HARDEN-004 Pitch Pack Notes

**Hardening Item:** HARDEN-004
**Pitch:** Starter Complete + Optimaks OS Basic
**Audience:** Founder-led prospect, partner, or internal review

## One-Line Positioning

Optimaks MVP01 demonstrates a clear Starter workflow for a service business, supported by a lightweight Optimaks OS Basic view for demo readiness and handoff.

## Problem

Small service businesses often manage requests, estimates, bookings, payments, and follow-ups across disconnected messages and manual notes.

## MVP01 Story

```text
Customer request
-> Owner review
-> Lead and customer context
-> Quote estimate
-> Booking
-> Payment status
-> Reminder and basic value snapshot
-> Optimaks OS Basic demo overview
```

## What To Emphasize

- The customer sees a clear request journey.
- The owner sees the next practical action across the Starter flow.
- The founder can present sample client readiness and route access from one overview.
- The demo is intentionally static, local, and safe for controlled review.
- Production capabilities remain separate decisions.

## Included

- Aircon / Home Service Starter Template presentation.
- Customer request, business workspace, and demo overview routes.
- Starter Complete workflow presentation.
- Sample client, plan label, readiness label, and preview-link meaning.
- Founder demo and release-readiness checklists.

## Reserved

- Production admin console and client backend.
- Tenant provisioning and account management.
- Billing, subscriptions, and plan enforcement.
- Deployment automation and module entitlements.
- MVP02 planning or execution without separate approval.

## Close

MVP01 is suitable for a founder-led demonstration of the product direction and operating flow. It is not a claim of production persistence, automation, integration, or administrative capability.
