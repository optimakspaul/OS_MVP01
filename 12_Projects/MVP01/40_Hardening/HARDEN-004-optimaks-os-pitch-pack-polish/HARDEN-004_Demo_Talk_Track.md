# HARDEN-004 Demo Talk Track

**Hardening Item:** HARDEN-004
**Target Duration:** 8 to 12 minutes
**Mode:** Founder-led static demonstration

## 1. Open With The Problem

Say:

> A service request often begins in a message, but the owner still needs a clear path to review the customer, prepare an estimate, confirm a booking, track payment, and follow up.

Open `/end-customer`.

Show the request details, manual next-step explanation, and preview boundary.

## 2. Move To The Owner Flow

Open `/workspace`.

Say:

> Starter Complete gives the owner one guided view of the practical workflow, from request review through follow-up and a basic value snapshot.

Walk through one sample customer. Keep the emphasis on the next owner action, not every panel.

## 3. Close In Optimaks OS Basic

Open `/optimaks-os`.

Say:

> Optimaks OS Basic is the founder's demo and readiness overview. The client names are examples, the plan and onboarding values are labels, and the links open the static demo routes.

Show:

```text
Pitch in three points
Sample demo records
Four founder-demo signals
Demo and release checklists
Included and reserved boundary
```

## 4. State The Boundary

Say:

> MVP01 is accepted for founder-led demonstration as Starter Complete + Optimaks OS Basic. It does not include a production admin console, real client backend, provisioning, billing, deployment automation, entitlements, or MVP02 execution.

## 5. End With The Decision

Say:

> This demo validates the product story and workflow. Any move into production capability or a future MVP requires a separate approved workpack.

## Presenter Guardrails

Do not claim that the demo:

```text
Stores real client records
Creates tenants or accounts
Controls plan access
Processes subscriptions or payments
Deploys client workspaces
Runs production onboarding
Starts MVP02
```
