# HARDEN-002 End Customer Copy Notes

**Hardening Item:** HARDEN-002
**Route:** `/end-customer`
**Scope:** Customer-facing copy polish only

---

## Copy Goal

Make the preview understandable without founder narration while avoiding any suggestion that the page submits a real request or provides automated service.

## Changes

### Hero

The hero now leads with the customer outcome:

```text
Get clear next steps for your home service request
```

It explains that the business reviews the request and guides the customer manually. Supporting reassurance states that the preview needs no account, collects no payment, and sends no automatic messages.

### Preview Boundary

The notice now starts with:

```text
Preview only: nothing is sent or saved
```

It explicitly excludes business contact, lead or booking creation, payment processing, and reminder scheduling.

### Request Flow

The page explains three steps:

```text
Describe the service issue
The business reviews the request
Confirm the next step together
```

The form introduction and preferred-timing label now describe a first conversation rather than implying a confirmed booking.

### Calls To Action

```text
Review my request preview
Open WhatsApp draft
Continue the demo in the business workspace
```

Each CTA states its actual effect. The WhatsApp copy explains that no recipient is configured and the customer chooses whether to send the draft.

### Commercial and Follow-up Boundaries

The estimate, booking, payment, and reminder cards now use explicit manual-only wording:

```text
No instant or binding quote
No reserved or scheduled slot
No payment handling
No scheduled reminder
```

## Copy Safety Result

The copy describes a static customer-journey preview. It does not promise real submission, pricing, availability, payment, messaging, or follow-up automation.
