# HARDEN-005 Founder Demo Runbook

**Project:** MVP01
**Demo Positioning:** Starter Complete + Optimaks OS Basic
**Target Duration:** 8 to 12 minutes
**Demo Mode:** Static, local, non-persistent

## Before The Demo

1. Run or open the current MVP01 preview.
2. Confirm `/end-customer`, `/workspace`, and `/optimaks-os` load.
3. Use sample information only.
4. Keep the included/reserved boundary visible.
5. Do not describe preview controls as production operations.

## 1. Customer Problem

Open `/end-customer`.

Say:

> A service request often starts in a message. This page shows how the customer can provide the key details needed for a useful first response.

Show:

```text
Service issue
Service area and contact details
Preferred timing
Manual next-step explanation
Local request preview
Optional WhatsApp draft
```

Boundary statement:

> Nothing is sent or saved. No quote, booking, payment, or reminder is created.

## 2. Owner Workflow

Use the route navigation to open `/workspace`.

Say:

> Starter Complete gives the owner a guided view of the practical workflow and the next manual action.

Follow one sample customer through:

```text
Request
Lead
Customer
Quote estimate
Booking
Payment status
Settings
Reminder and basic value snapshot
```

Boundary statement:

> The controls update only this browser preview. They do not create records, move money, schedule work, or send messages.

## 3. Founder Closeout

Open `/optimaks-os`.

Say:

> Optimaks OS Basic is the founder's demo and readiness overview. Its client, plan, and onboarding values are sample labels rather than live account data.

Show:

```text
Three-point pitch
Sample demo records
Founder demo checklist
Release-readiness checklist
Included and reserved boundary
```

## 4. Close The Pitch

Say:

> MVP01 demonstrates the Starter Complete workflow and a lightweight Optimaks OS Basic overview. Production persistence, client administration, provisioning, billing, deployment, entitlements, and MVP02 remain separate approved decisions.

## Presenter Guardrails

Do not claim:

```text
Customer or business data is saved
The WhatsApp draft is sent automatically
Quotes, bookings, invoices, receipts, or reports are generated
Payments are processed
Reminders are scheduled
PayNow is functional
Optimaks OS Basic is a production admin console
MVP02 planning or execution has started
```

## Recovery

If a local preview control becomes confusing, reload the route and continue with the static sample state. No production data can be affected.
