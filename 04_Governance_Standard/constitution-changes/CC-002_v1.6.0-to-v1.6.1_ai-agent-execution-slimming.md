# CC-002 v1.6.0 to v1.6.1 AI Agent Execution Slimming

**Change ID:** CC-002  
**From Version:** v1.6.0  
**To Version:** v1.6.1  
**Change Level:** Patch  
**Effective Date:** 2026-05-19  
**Founder Decision:** Adopt v1.6.1 as active constitution.

---

## 1. Reason

v1.6.0 became complete enough for company governance, but too large for daily AI issue execution.

AI tools need a smaller, clearer entrypoint and a controlled active standards index so they do not load strategic, archived, or superseded documents during normal coding.
